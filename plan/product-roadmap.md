# Product Roadmap

An outcome-focused product roadmap is the ranked set of changes we intend to make in what our customers and users can do, held in horizons of confidence (Now, Next, Later). It is re-ranked from evidence as we learn, never fixed as a plan.

- Outcomes only, never outputs.
- An outcome is one thing a customer or user can newly do in their work, made possible by what we ship.
- One heading line per outcome: who, and what they can newly do; under it, one line of the evidence that will show they do it.
- No features, solutions or product internals, no reasoning.
- Now is ordered by expected completion; Next and Later by priority.
- No dependencies; anyone may work any outcome at any time.
- Re-rank whenever evidence arrives.
- Delete an outcome when its evidence shows it is achieved.
