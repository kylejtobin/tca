# Roadmap

An outcome-focused roadmap is the ranked set of changes we intend to make in what parties outside the system can do, held in horizons of confidence (Now, Next, Later). It is re-ranked from evidence as we learn, never fixed as a plan.

- Outcomes only, never outputs.
- An outcome is one thing a party outside the system can newly do across one crossing, made possible by what we build.
- One heading line per outcome: the party, and what it can newly do.
- No product internals, no code, no reasoning.
- Now is ordered by expected completion; Next and Later by priority.
- No dependencies; anyone may work any outcome at any time.
- Re-rank after every task.
- Delete an outcome when it is achieved.
