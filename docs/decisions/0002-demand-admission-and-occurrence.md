# 0002. Demand, admission and occurrence: the contract after the review of 0001

**Status:** experiments, 2026-10-23, revised the same day after a second and a third review. Supersedes in part [`0001-durable-program-contract.md`](0001-durable-program-contract.md). This record bounds every claim by the test that executes it; where a claim is wider than its tests, it says so. It does not resolve the design questions in `TCA_ARCHITECTURAL_FOUNDATIONS.md` §18, and it does not mark any outcome in `TODO.md` or any requirement in `REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md` as met. Nothing here is a durable-host demonstration.

**Evidence:** `packages/python/type-construction-program`, tests under `tests/type_construction_program/`. Business declarations: `fulfillment.py`, `auction.py`, `review.py`, `lines.py`, `decisions.py`, `transfer.py`, `composite.py`. Executed with `just check` under the locked dependencies: ruff, ruff format, basedpyright strict, bandit, and pytest, 103 tests passing, of which 84 are in this package and 19 in `type-construction-world`.

## What was wrong in 0001, as assumptions rather than defects

The review of 0001 found that the first slice made its examples executable by weakening the architecture they were meant to test. Grouped by the assumption each disproved case rests on:

1. **Available and demanded was taken for justified.** The evaluator merged the open work of every premise of a conjunction and of every member of an alternative and dispatched the union. Foundations §7.2 says decisions precede their dependent effects, with or without a fallback, and §7.3 says a choice between effects is never guessed. The fulfillment fixture happened to sequence its charge before its shipment by data dependency, so the defect never showed.
2. **A returned payload was taken for evidence.** `perform` validated a returned result against the interaction's records and recorded it, without the correlation, duplicate, closure or intent checks a delivery went through. Foundations §4.2, §6.2 and §10.4: a result is validated and admitted before it is evidence, and type validity is not source authority.
3. **A class was taken to have one construction relationship.** Evidence was located, memoized and bound by class. Two roles of one class in one input were refused with the instruction to declare two classes. Foundations §4.1 and §5: a class is not an occurrence, and a construction relationship is how a particular occurrence obtains its premises.
4. **A correlated result arriving before any request was taken to satisfy the premise.** A `Captured` for the right order, from the right source, admitted when this invocation had asked for nothing. That is available evidence mistaken for the declared authority, which is the provider's answer to this program's request.

Each is replaced below with the rule that repairs it, the test that would fail without the repair, and the neighbouring cases tried against the replacement.

## What was wrong in the first revision of this record

The second review found that the replacements were tested one at a time, inside declarations shaped to exercise each alone, and that two of the reviewer's regressions were left out. Traced through declaration, compilation, evaluation, admission and closure, each remaining defect was a place where the representation dropped information or an assumption stood in for it:

1. **A delivery identity lost its source.** Admission compared the origin alone, so one `DeliveryId` at two sources was one claim and the second payload a conflict (foundations §9.1: source and id together identify a delivery). Regression: `test_a_delivery_identity_is_scoped_to_its_source`.
2. **A request node was keyed by class.** The compiler cached the request node by the request class, so one operation demanded under two bound collections was compiled once, under the first. Regression: `test_one_operation_under_two_collections_is_a_request_per_line_of_each`. The request is now compiled where its receive is demanded, and admission derives from the committed intents, not from a class-keyed flag, whether an unnamed result must correlate to an element (`test_an_unnamed_result_for_a_per_line_request_is_not_tied_by_count`).
3. **A collection source lost its element.** What a source had said, and whether its closer was admitted, were read for the whole invocation, so a per-line collection held every line's records and one line's closure sealed every line. Regression: `test_closing_one_lines_review_leaves_the_other_lines_open_and_their_evidence_apart`; `test_a_selection_per_line_is_decided_by_that_lines_closure_alone`.
4. **Correlations were judged one at a time.** A correlation that held at two elements was refused as ambiguous before the next one could narrow it, so a relationship unique only as a whole was inexpressible. Regression: `test_a_correlation_unique_only_as_a_whole_ties_the_record_to_one_line`. The element is now the one at which every declared equality holds together.
5. **Only TCA's own kinds counted as undecided.** A class with an ordinary Pydantic validator or a computed field was treated as certain to construct, so an effect beside it was dispatched before the validator ran (foundations §8.2). Regression: `test_an_ordinary_validator_over_delayed_evidence_defers_the_dependent_effect`. The second revision replaced this with a recognition rule over decorators and TCA computations and stated that a class outside the rule constructs from valid premises; the third review showed that false with `Field(min_length=2)` and an `Annotated` after-validator. See the next section.
6. **Premises were supplied by alias.** The evaluator constructed a class by field name through a validator that accepts aliases only, so a request or a fact with a native alias never constructed. Regression: `test_native_aliases_on_a_request_and_a_fact_keep_their_meaning`.

## What was wrong in the second revision

The third review found the governing principle still untested: a second recognition rule had replaced the first, with a false sentence beside it. Each of its four findings is a regression now:

1. **Construction was predicted, not performed.** `Field(min_length=2)` on a tuple field and `Annotated[..., AfterValidator(...)]` on a field are native validation the recognition rule did not see, so the dependent shipment was dispatched before either ran. Regressions: `test_a_native_field_constraint_over_an_unclosed_source_defers_the_dependent_effect`, `test_an_annotated_after_validator_over_delayed_evidence_defers_the_dependent_effect`. The rule is gone: every class constructed from an open premise is undecided, because its construction is a Pydantic validation the evaluator does not interpret and knows to hold only once it has.
2. **Contradictory bindings were ordered, not refused.** Two bindings of one premise to different places were accepted and the first written won. Regression: `test_two_bindings_of_one_premise_to_different_places_are_refused_not_ordered`; both an `Interaction` and a `Program` refuse at declaration, naming both places.
3. **Construction by name left alias reading on.** With `by_name=True` and alias validation still active, a field whose alias is another field's name took that field's premise, while the recorded occurrence still named its own: a wrong value with the right provenance. Regression: `test_a_premise_bound_by_name_is_not_swapped_by_another_fields_alias`; construction is by name only (`by_alias=False, by_name=True`).
4. **Constants bypassed the structured path.** A default-only class was constructed with a bare call, so a default its own constraint refuses escaped as an exception. Regression: `test_a_constant_whose_default_its_constraint_refuses_blocks_with_a_structured_reason`; a constant is constructed inside the same validated path and a refused default is `Malformed`.

## The construction graph

`Program` now compiles the goal into a graph of construction relationships, `Node`: `Found` (a value at a location in the input or a received record), `Heard` (one record a source says, with the node of the request it answers), `Made` (a class from its premises), `Choice` (a union), `Each` (one construction per element of a collection) and `All` (every record a collection source says, sealed by its closer). Nodes are structural: two places that obtain a value the same way compile to one node and share one occurrence; two that obtain it differently are two nodes. Evaluation memoizes by node and the scope it varies over, not by class.

- Executable: `test_pure_construction_per_line_shares_each_lines_occurrence` (one line total shared by the priced line and the sum); `test_each_role_is_its_own_occurrence_and_its_own_operation` (one `Account` class, two nodes, two operations); `test_each_total_is_over_its_own_lines_and_equal_lines_are_two`.
- Not demonstrated: that memoization bounds evaluation cost (acceptance A7 speaks of cost as well as sharing). No test measures it.

## Binding: the declaration that tells two places apart

`Binding(premise, subject)` declares that the premise at a path is obtained at a place in the accepted input. On an `Interaction` the path is from the request it sends; on a `Program` it is from the goal. A premise that is a tuple of a constructed class is bound to the collection it is one construction per element of. Inside one construction per element, a class found in that element is the element's; that is what "per element" means, and it is the one place the compiler prefers a location, stated here so it is not a silent rule. A class found at two places the declarations do not tell apart is still refused, and the refusal now names both places and the `Binding` that would decide, instead of a class to invent.

- Executable: `test_two_bindings_of_one_premise_to_different_places_are_refused_not_ordered`; `test_a_premise_bound_by_name_is_not_swapped_by_another_fields_alias` (the value and its recorded occurrence agree); `test_without_a_binding_two_roles_of_one_class_are_refused_naming_the_binding`; `test_each_role_is_its_own_occurrence_and_its_own_operation` (the debit request holds the source account, the credit request the target, each with its own identity and its own correlated confirmation); `test_a_confirmation_for_the_other_role_does_not_answer_this_one`; `test_a_binding_to_a_place_holding_another_class_is_refused`; `test_without_a_binding_one_element_class_in_two_collections_is_refused`; `test_each_total_is_over_its_own_lines_and_equal_lines_are_two`; `test_a_class_held_at_two_sites_is_refused_naming_the_binding_that_would_decide`.
- Bounded: a binding's subject is a place in the accepted input. Binding a premise to a value in received evidence is not expressible. A goal-relative binding into a union member or a request is accepted by the path grammar and exercised by no test.

## Demand: two rules over the graph

**Necessity across alternatives.** Where more than one member of a union is live (not refuted), a request is dispatched only if every live member needs it. A request one member needs and another does not is withheld as `Unchosen`. When nothing else can advance the invocation, that is `Blocked` with `Undecidable`, naming the premise, the members and the operations: the declarations have not said which effect to perform.

**Decisions precede dependent effects.** A premise is undecided while evidence still to come may refute it, or while its own construction may: a receive whose source may answer with another member; any class constructed from an open premise; a tuple over an unclosed source whose element is such a class. The second is the boundary between Pydantic and the evaluator: a construction is a Pydantic validation, with whatever constraints, validators, computed fields and declared conditions the class carries, and the evaluator does not interpret the schema to predict it (foundations §8.2, §16). It knows a construction holds once it has performed it, and not before. While any premise of a conjunction is undecided, the requests under its decided premises are withheld as `Deferred`, naming the premises they wait on, and only the requests under the undecided premises are dispatched. This holds with and without a fallback outcome. What is decided while open is what is not constructed: a value found in admitted evidence, and a receive with one declared answer whose request is available. Two such requests dispatch together (`test_two_necessary_requests_nothing_can_refute_are_dispatched_together`), so this is causal ordering, not serialization of everything; a request beside a fact that wraps another request's result does wait for that result, and this record does not claim that cost is always acceptable.

Both are properties of how outcomes combine at `Made`, `Each` and `Choice`, not of any one request; a `Frontier` reports what is withheld and why beside what is dispatched and awaited.

- Executable, decisions first: `test_a_constructible_demanded_request_waits_for_the_decision_it_depends_on` (no fallback: the ping waits for the registrar); `test_a_denial_blocks_and_the_dependent_request_was_never_dispatched`; `test_with_a_fallback_the_branch_not_taken_performs_no_effect`; `test_a_decision_nothing_is_sent_for_still_defers_the_request_that_depends_on_it` (the decision is an unsolicited receive); `test_two_operations_with_equal_request_bodies_have_different_identities` (the hold waits for the charge, which may decline).
- Executable, never guessed: `test_a_choice_between_two_effects_nothing_decides_is_not_guessed` (`Blocked` with `Undecidable`, no intent committed); `test_the_same_choice_decided_by_a_rating_performs_the_one_the_rating_picks`.
- Executable, not serialized: `test_two_necessary_requests_nothing_can_refute_are_dispatched_together`; `test_two_decisions_are_made_together`; `test_work_per_element_of_a_source_proceeds_before_closure`; `test_a_deciding_request_that_needs_another_result_has_that_result_obtained_first` (in this one case deferral does not stall, because the request that decides needs the pinged result and so the ping is under the undecided premise; no general claim that deferral cannot stall is made or tested).
- Executable, native validation: `test_an_ordinary_validator_over_delayed_evidence_defers_the_dependent_effect` (a `model_validator`), `test_an_annotated_after_validator_over_delayed_evidence_defers_the_dependent_effect` (an `Annotated` after-validator on a field), `test_a_native_field_constraint_over_an_unclosed_source_defers_the_dependent_effect` (`Field(min_length=2)` over a closed source). In each the shipment waits, and a refusing construction blocks as `Malformed` with the shipment never dispatched. `test_a_constant_whose_default_its_constraint_refuses_blocks_with_a_structured_reason` shows a refused default takes the same path. Bounded: a native refusal is reported as `Malformed`, not as a refutation, since only a declared `Refutable` says which refusals are its condition; whether that is the right classification for a business validator is not decided. These four are the native forms tried; no claim is made for forms not tried, and none is needed, since the rule does not depend on recognizing them.
- Executable, eligibility: `test_a_consumer_added_cannot_weaken_what_the_request_requires` (A2); `test_a_justified_effect_stays_in_the_history_when_later_work_blocks` (A6).
- Counterexample that bounds the rule, kept as a limit: `test_a_passive_alternative_beside_the_charge_leaves_the_charge_unchosen`. Shipped or refused or cancelled by the customer: the charge is needed by two members and not by the third, which needs only a delivery that may never come. The evaluator withholds the charge and waits. The declarations do not say the charge is attempted while a cancellation may arrive, and shipping cannot be the outcome while one may: that needs a boundary on the cancellation (foundations §9.4), and no declaration here expresses absence over a closed receive. Whether that is the right reading of the program, or a missing declaration kind, is not decided here.

## Admission: one judgement for a delivery and a returned result

`admissible(program, invocation, claim)` is the one place a claim becomes evidence. A delivery and a result an attempt returns are both a `Claim`: the source, how it came (`Delivered` or `Returned`), the payload, and the request it answers when known. The judgement is the same: the receive is selected by the request named or by the source's declared receives; the payload is validated as what that receive says; a claim already admitted under its source and origin is one claim and a different payload under them is a `Conflict`; every declared correlation holds, and where subjects enter a collection, at exactly one element together; a claim for a solicited receive answers one committed request, named or the one its correlation selects, and with none it is `Unrequested`; a claim with no element for an interaction whose committed requests are per element is `Uncorrelated`, not tied to whichever request is left; a receive that says nothing more refuses, where a collection source is closed for an element by a closer correlated to that element, or for the invocation by one correlated to none. `perform` records the attempt's outcome only after the result passes; refused, nothing is recorded, and what that makes of the attempt is the host's to record.

- Executable: `test_a_result_for_another_order_is_refused_whichever_way_it_comes` (the same `Uncorrelated` reason by delivery and by return); `test_a_result_this_invocation_never_asked_for_is_not_evidence` (`Unrequested`, and the request is still dispatched); `test_a_result_delivered_before_the_attempt_is_the_answer_the_attempt_then_repeats` (early callback after the intent is committed: admitted as the answer; the attempt's equal result is that answer; a different one is `Closed` both ways); `test_a_result_for_the_other_line_does_not_answer_this_lines_request` (an element-correlated result that names one line's request and correlates to the other is `Ambiguous` both ways; unnamed, it answers the line it correlates to).
- Executable, in combination: `test_a_delivery_identity_is_scoped_to_its_source`; `test_a_correlation_unique_only_as_a_whole_ties_the_record_to_one_line` (and with the sku alone declared, the same record is `Ambiguous` between the two lines that share it); `test_an_unnamed_result_for_a_per_line_request_is_not_tied_by_count`; `test_closing_one_lines_review_leaves_the_other_lines_open_and_their_evidence_apart` (an approval for a closed line is refused while another line still takes them).
- Carried from 0001: the admission experiments in `test_admission.py`, and `test_a_receipt_shaped_like_the_result_is_told_from_it_by_report_not_by_shape`.
- Bounded: these are contract experiments over an in-memory history. They do not demonstrate N3 to N6 against a host.

## What the §18 questions look like after this

Not resolved. What the experiments show about each:

- **Q1, one interaction kind.** One declaration kind still expresses the cases in `test_interactions.py`: immediate and delayed results with one business outcome, an unsolicited receive, a timer. The admission revision narrows the kind: a solicited receive is satisfied only by an answer to a request this invocation committed. No experiment shows the kind is sufficient for an interaction not represented here.
- **Q2, three results.** `Completed`, `Frontier`, `Blocked` still express the four conditions in `test_evaluation.py`, and `Frontier` now carries withheld work with its reasons. `Blocked` gains `Undecidable`. Whether a withheld frontier with nothing awaited should ever be reported as waiting rather than blocked is settled here as blocked for unchosen effects; a deferred-only frontier with nothing awaited cannot arise, and the evaluator raises if it does.
- **Q3, effect identity.** Invocation, request class, and the occurrence of the request, where an occurrence is now a node of the graph bound in a scope. `test_identity.py`, `test_occurrences.py` and `test_roles.py` show distinct operations for byte-equal bodies, for equal elements, and for two roles of one class. Logical repetition without a distinguishing premise remains inexpressible: two intended operations with the same request node and the same scope are one.
- **Q4, closure.** `test_closure.py` as in 0001, and closure per element: a collection source correlated into a collection is one collection per element, each closed by its own closer, and a selection over it is decided by that closure alone (`test_closing_one_lines_review_leaves_the_other_lines_open_and_their_evidence_apart`, `test_a_selection_per_line_is_decided_by_that_lines_closure_alone`). The passive-alternative limit above is where closure meets demand and is not resolved.

## Unresolved

- Absence over a closed receive, and with it the passive alternative beside an effect (above).
- Correlation or binding to a value in received evidence, such as a provider reference from a receipt.
- Nested collections: the compiler and the scope admit a collection inside a collection; the per-line approvals are a collection source inside an input collection and are exercised, but a tuple field inside a tuple element is not. Goal-relative bindings into union members and requests are accepted by the path grammar and exercised by no test.
- A tuple of a class found at two places without a binding on the tuple: the compiler discovers the collection a tuple follows by compiling its element once unbound, and a class found at two places is refused in that pass, so the binding on the tuple is required there even where the element's own premises would decide it. The refusal names the binding. Whether discovery should resolve it is not decided.
- Role trust: a receipt with the shape of a result is told apart only by the capability's report (foundations §11.5).
- A returned result that fails admission: the attempt happened and returned something that is not evidence; this protocol records nothing and leaves the host to record `uncertain`. Untested.
- Completion and cancellation of an invocation; program version in the identity; evaluation cost.
