# Measure Outcome

State the overall outcome you are pursuing: the reason the work exists.
Then write three lines, in this order:

```
Did:     what changed in the world. Nouns and verbs. One line.
Learned: the one fact you did not have before that bears on what to do next.
Moved:   closer | nowhere | backward, followed by the change in the overall
         outcome's state that proves it.
```

## Gotchas

- You will substitute the task's goal for the overall outcome. "Deleted the
  files" is not an outcome; the reason the files were deleted is.
- You will cite finishing as evidence. Completion is not movement. Without a
  change in the outcome's state, Moved is "nowhere".
- You will write a verdict first and fit evidence to it. That is why Moved is
  last.
- The task that felt most careful, thorough or complete is the one most likely
  to have served the plan instead of the outcome. Read its Moved hardest.
