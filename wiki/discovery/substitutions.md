# Substitutions

Every discovery failure has one mechanism. A decision has not been made, a token has to land, and what lands is an available artifact standing where the decision should be. The artifact is generable without deciding. That is why it is reached for. The construct pages list forbidden shapes at the level of code; this page lists them at the level of thinking.

| Substitution | What it looks like | The move |
|---|---|---|
| **A procedure** | Asked to describe the thinking, the model produces a build process: render, read, name, gate, commit, stop. The middle step, deciding what the evidence means for the world, becomes "nulls become unions, vocabularies become axes." | A procedure is what gets written when a decision has not been made. Answer the question that was asked. |
| **A vendor's list** | A published taxonomy of 104 categories fetched and adopted as the domain's vocabulary. A `removed` list in a sync reply adopted as a state a transaction passes through. | That is the source's account. Decide the vocabulary from the thing and what the program does with it. Alias theirs at the crossing. |
| **A lookup** | A grep across a skill for keywords, presented as "the register sized this vocabulary," when no register exists. | A search result is not a decision. If the answer is not known, reason from the thing until it is. |
| **The checker** | The type checker refuses the whitelist's exemplar form, so the exemplar is rewritten to what the checker accepts, and the standard is edited to match. | The form is the standard. Name the checker's blindness where it bites, as a recorded exception. Never edit the whitelist. |
| **The operator** | "Whether `Money` may be signed is yours to rule on." "Merchants gain identity later." A decision the modeler could make, handed upstream. | Nothing is the operator's to rule on. The rules decide. A conflict means the thing is unseen; look harder. |
| **Prose** | An essay in place of the filled schema. Question 9 answered as "two things may need another look." A whole-set review delivered as "it holds." | Written answers in the form the step demands. A hedge has no slot in a schema. A pass is one line per entry per break. |
| **A test** | Twenty-seven tests asserting that Pydantic refuses an extra key, admits a literal, selects a discriminator. Validation moved from a validator into a test suite. | A substrate fact is carried by the shape that depends on it. If the substrate changed, construction would refuse and the gate would say so. Tests are for the program's own meaning. |

## The tell

Each substitution has a tell, and the tell is the same: the output looks finished and no decision is visible in it. A procedure has steps but no shape. A vendor list has values but no reason. A grep has hits but no judgment. A suppression has a green gate but no named gap. A kicked question has a question mark. Prose has a sentence that explains. A test has an assertion about the substrate instead of a type that carries it.

When the model catches itself reaching for one of these, the reach is the signal that the thinking has not been done. Stop and do it. Every substitution in the record cost more work than the decision would have: one cost a full rebuild, one cost an edit to the standard, one cost a total deletion of eight commits.

## Why the shape of the step prevents them

A procedure can be generated without deciding. A filled `name / is / must / holds` cannot: every field demands a decision and an empty field is visible. That is the whole reason [things](./things.md) and [constructs](./constructs.md) end in a schema and not a summary, and why the process runs one question per turn with the operator advancing. The substitutions are what a sequential generator produces when the graph decision has not been made and nothing forces it to be. The schema forces it.
