# Deciding

Discovery is the work of deciding. Every vocabulary, every shape, every conflict is settled by reasoning about the thing as it is in the world and what the program is for. Handing a decision to anything else is not caution. It is billing for thinking that was not done.

## Nothing is the operator's to rule on

The operator holds authority and acceptance. The operator does not hold the answers. "Whether `Money` may be signed is yours to rule on" and "merchants gain identity later when recurrence needs it" are the same move: a decision the modeler could make, kicked upstream so the modeler is not the one who made it. The rules decide. A signed sum carrying a direction is the fused break, so `Money` is a magnitude and direction is its own axis, and no one has to be asked.

## A conflict is an unseen thing

When two models cannot both be right, the world is not in conflict with itself. The modeler has not yet seen the thing. The answer is found by looking harder until the shape that makes both true appears.

A transaction's other side was `PartyId`. A transfer between the user's own accounts has no party on the other side. Neither model was wrong; the thing was unseen: the other side of a movement is a party or one of the user's own accounts, a union on one axis, and a transfer is one movement seen from both sides. The conflict named the missing structure.

Institution carried an identifier; merchant and income source carried only a name. A union of unlike members is not a union of one axis. Looking harder: a party is one kind of thing with identity, and institution, payee, payer are roles it plays in relations that already exist. Apple holds a savings account and sells phones. One party, two relations. The asymmetry named the missing decision.

## Nature decides shape; purpose decides vocabulary where nature has none

What a thing is, what it is made of, what must be true of it: these are read off the world. A mortgage is always secured by real property. A student loan is never secured. A vehicle secures an auto loan whether or not this reply happened to include one; the reply's silence is the source's, not the world's.

What a movement of money was for has no shape in nature. Its vocabulary is set by what the program does: income for a means test, debt payments to creditors, and spending. Nothing finer is needed by any use the program has, so nothing finer exists. A vendor's 104-value category taxonomy is one source's evidence for which of ours a movement belongs to, resolved at the crossing, the way a vendor's account subtype resolves to one of ours.

Structure comes before vocabulary here too. "Transfer" as a purpose duplicates a transaction whose counterparty is an own account. "Institution charge" duplicates a transaction whose counterparty is the account's own institution. Purpose keeps only what structure cannot already say.

## The whitelist is the pattern

The construct pages are a whitelist, and anything not on it is forbidden. The whitelist is the fifteen constructs and each page's allowed and forbidden forms. It is not a vocabulary of names, and its primitive list is not a closed set of atoms. A scalar over `AwareDatetime` and a scalar over `UUID7` are the semantic scalar pattern: one value space, one constraint, proven at construction. Offering `str` with a regex to stay inside a literal reading of the primitive list is modeling the primitive to avoid the work, which is the forbidden thing wearing a whitelist costume. Model everything: for every noun the evidence shows, pick the value space in which the wrong value cannot be built.

## The written pass

No step passes on judgment. A step's output is a written artifact in the form the step demands. For the breaks check that means one line per entry per break, stating why it does not break. A pass that is not written was not run: "I read the whole set and it holds" is the modeler's confidence, and the design that produced the category taxonomy and the `removed` state had been read whole and passed.
