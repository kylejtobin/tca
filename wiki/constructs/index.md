# Constructs

The thirteen declaration forms, the state-transition shape, and the composition-root site. Every example inhabits the venue world decided on the [definition](../doctrine/definition.md) page and is correct to copy verbatim. Naming, construction rules, and substrate facts apply to every page.

## Pages

- [naming](./naming.md): How every structure is named for the domain thing or fact it carries.
- [construction-rules](./construction-rules.md): Construction is the proof; there is no validation stage, and the dependency graph determines order.
- [substrate-claims](./substrate-claims.md): The Pydantic facts the doctrine rests on, each established by a run against the pinned version.
- [semantic-scalar](./semantic-scalar.md): One atomic meaning over a primitive or a closed value space.
- [value-object](./value-object.md): A frozen identityless product whose meaning is exhausted by field equality.
- [concept-model](./concept-model.md): A frozen complete domain thing, durable fact, or refinement; the class is the kind.
- [union](./union.md): A closed sum of alternatives on one semantic axis, each carrying its own facts.
- [ordered-union](./ordered-union.md): Attempt-order construction, admitted only where the strong variant's sole failure means the declared fallback.
- [collection](./collection.md): A frozen typed sequence with meaning of its own; keyed association has no admitted substrate.
- [transformation](./transformation.md): A pure implication from proven inputs to a constructed output, within a closed one-expression algebra.
- [foreign-model](./foreign-model.md): Another system's representation lifted whole into a frozen typed boundary model.
- [contract-model](./contract-model.md): This program's published request or reply, carrying exactly the decided wire facts.
- [config](./config.md): Environment input constructed once into a frozen settings model, lax on input because the environment is text.
- [route](./route.md): A frozen model of one transport crossing, constructing ingress and projecting egress.
- [effect-interpreter](./effect-interpreter.md): Execution of one typed action through one imported capability, constructing the observed outcome.
- [action](./action.md): A frozen value describing one intended external effect without executing it.
- [state-transition](./state-transition.md): The concept-model shape whose self-typed prior field represents immutable succession.
- [composition-root](./composition-root.md): The site where a framework callback evaluates the per-input terminal expression.
