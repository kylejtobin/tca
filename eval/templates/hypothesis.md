# Experiment Design

- **Hypothesis:** <the change and its predicted effect on the primary metric>.
- **Treatment:** <the exact change applied>.
- **Control:** <the condition without the change>.
- **Randomization unit:** <the unit assigned to treatment or control>.
- **Triggering:** <the condition under which a unit enters the analysis>.
- **Primary metric (OEC):** <the single metric the decision rests on>.
- **Secondary metrics:** <metrics reported, not decided on>.
- **Guardrail metrics:** <metrics that must not degrade>.
- **Minimum detectable effect:** <the smallest change worth detecting>.
- **Sample size and power:** <the units needed for the chosen power at the chosen significance level>.
- **Significance level:** <α>.
- **Duration:** <how long the experiment runs, or how many units it collects>.
- **Analysis method:** <the statistical test or model>.
- **Validity checks:** <the sample-ratio-mismatch check and other checks of trustworthiness>.
- **Decision criteria:** <the results that lead to ship, don't ship, or rerun>.
