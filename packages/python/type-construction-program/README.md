# type-construction-program

Business programs as declared external relationships, the construction graph compiled from them, the recorded history of one invocation, and an experimental pure evaluation of the next justified work. This package is the executable form of the contract experiments under outcome 1 of [`TODO.md`](../../../TODO.md). It is experimental machinery for those experiments, not the replacement runtime, and the experiments do not resolve the design questions they exercise.

## What is here

- **Declarations** (`declaration`). `Source` names an external party. `Interaction` is one declared relationship with a source: what it says (`receives`), what this program sends to make it say so (`sends`), the receipt it may answer the send with first (`acknowledges`), how a delivery is tied to the invocation (`correlated`), where a premise of the request is obtained when its class alone does not say (`bound`), and the evidence after which it says nothing more (`closed_by`). `Binding` is that last declaration: a premise is obtained at a place in the accepted input, or a tuple follows a collection there. `Program` is a goal, the input it accepts, its interactions and its bindings; constructing one compiles the goal into a graph of construction relationships, `Node`, and refuses a program whose premise is obtained no way or more than one way, naming the places and the binding that would tell them apart.
- **History and protocol** (`invocation`). `Invocation` is an append-only history: the accepted input, admitted `Evidence`, committed `Intended` effects and numbered `Attempted` outcomes. An occurrence is where a value comes from: a `Site`, a `Built` or `Elements`. `EffectIdentity` is the invocation, the request class and the occurrence of the request. `admissible` is the one judgement by which a claim becomes evidence, whether it came as a delivery (`admit`) or back inside an attempt (`perform`): selection by the request answered or the declared receive, validation, one claim per source and origin, every declared correlation holding together at one element, one answer per committed request, and closure, per element where the source is correlated into a collection. `acknowledge`, `failed` and `uncertain` record what else an attempt produced.
- **Evaluation** (`evaluation`). `evaluate(program, invocation)` returns `Completed`, `Frontier` or `Blocked`. A frontier holds the requests justified now, the receives outstanding, and the requests demanded but withheld: `Deferred` behind a premise that evidence still to come may refute or whose own construction may refuse (a declared condition, a validator, a computed field), or `Unchosen` because one live alternative needs it and another does not. An invocation whose only remaining work is unchosen is blocked as `Undecidable`. Construction is by node and scope, so two places that obtain a value the same way share one occurrence and two elements with equal content do not. It constructs pure values through Pydantic, reads no clock, performs no effect and allocates nothing not derived from the history.

Pydantic owns composition, constraints, aliases, unions, collections and validation. The declarations add only what Pydantic cannot state: the source of a record, its tie to an invocation, the closure of an evolving source, and which of two places a premise is obtained from.

## What is not here

A durable host. The history is in memory; nothing demonstrates recovery, worker ownership, stale commits, concurrency or early delivery against a store. Acceptance requirements N1 to N11 and N13 in [`docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../../../docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) need that host and remain unverified.

What each experiment demonstrates, what it does not, and what remains unresolved are in [`docs/decisions/0002-demand-admission-and-occurrence.md`](../../../docs/decisions/0002-demand-admission-and-occurrence.md), which supersedes in part [`0001`](../../../docs/decisions/0001-durable-program-contract.md).

The package needs no runtime configuration.
