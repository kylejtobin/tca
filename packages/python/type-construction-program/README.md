# type-construction-program

Business programs as declared external relationships, the construction graph compiled from them, the recorded history of one invocation, and an experimental pure evaluation of the next justified work. This package is the executable form of the contract experiments under outcome 1 of [`TODO.md`](../../../TODO.md). It is experimental machinery for those experiments, not the replacement runtime, and the experiments do not resolve the design questions they exercise.

## What is here

- **Declarations** (`declaration`). `Source` names an external party. `Interaction` is one declared relationship with a source: what it says (`receives`), what this program sends to make it say so (`sends`), the receipt it may answer the send with first (`acknowledges`), how a delivery is tied to the invocation (`correlated`), where a premise of the request is obtained when its class alone does not say (`bound`), and the evidence after which it says nothing more (`closed_by`). `Binding` is that last declaration: a premise is obtained at a place in the accepted input, or a tuple follows a collection there. `Program` is a goal, the input it accepts, its interactions and its bindings; constructing one compiles the goal into a graph of construction relationships, `Node`, and refuses a program whose premise is obtained no way or more than one way, naming the places and the binding that would tell them apart, and one whose bindings contradict each other.
- **History and protocol** (`invocation`). `Invocation` is an append-only history: the accepted input, admitted `Evidence`, committed `Intended` effects and numbered `Attempted` outcomes. An occurrence is where a value comes from: a `Site`, a `Built` or `Elements`. `EffectIdentity` is the invocation, the request class and the occurrence of the request. `admissible` is the one judgement by which a claim becomes evidence, whether it came as a delivery (`admit`) or back inside an attempt (`perform`): selection by the request answered or the declared receive, validation, one claim per source and origin, every declared correlation holding together at one element, one answer per committed request, and closure, per element where the source is correlated into a collection. `acknowledge`, `failed` and `uncertain` record what else an attempt produced. The history owns its values: it copies the accepted input and each committed request, so the caller's objects remain the caller's and changing them afterwards changes no recorded premise, evaluation or effect identity; what it admits from a payload it constructs itself; what the evaluator derives, requests and the goal, are constructions of its own.
- **Evaluation** (`evaluation`). `evaluate(program, invocation)` returns `Completed`, `Frontier` or `Blocked`. A frontier holds the requests justified now, the receives outstanding, and the requests demanded but withheld: `Deferred` behind judgements still to be made beside it, or `Unchosen` because one live alternative needs it and another does not. Each open premise carries the judgements still to be made on it, at the places they are declared (`Judgement`): `Decided`, a source that may answer otherwise or a refutable condition; `Bounded`, a native constraint at a field; `Unread`, anything in Pydantic's own resolved schema for the class or the field that runs code or depends on more than the value, a validator, a revalidated instance, a discriminator, a default. A judgement at a field or under a premise is over that premise; a judgement a class makes on the whole is over every premise. The requests under each premise stand or wait by their relation to every judgement open across the conjunction, derived from what each is over, never from a comparison between kinds of judgement: they decide a read judgement over their premise and stand, since independent decisions are made together; they depend on a read judgement over another premise and wait; and where an unread judgement leaves it open whether they decide or depend, under one reading they would stand and under another wait, they wait as unsettled. So the shipment beside a nested eligibility fact waits for the fact's validator; the same condition written on the goal that also holds the shipment leaves both requests waiting, since the evaluator can no longer read which premise the condition is over, and with nothing else to do that is reported as `Undetermined`, naming the judgement and the requests; written as a declared relation, nested or flat, the capture decides it and the shipment waits in both forms. `Deferred.behind` names what a request waits for and `Deferred.unread` the judgements that left its dependency unsettled. A native constraint is judged before its class constructs only with Pydantic's own schema for that field under the class's own configuration, and only when that schema contains no node that runs code or reads context, a field name or sibling data (`field_schema`): then the judgement is construction's own, made earlier. A field whose schema runs a function, a padding before-validator, a nested instance revalidated with a context-reading validator, is judged only by constructing, natively. Construction is by node and scope, so two places that obtain a value the same way share one occurrence and two elements with equal content do not. It constructs pure values through Pydantic, by field name only, from its own copy of each piece of evidence; a construction that alters its premise makes the evaluation `Blocked` as `Impure`. A validator that returns a replacement is native behaviour: the result holds the replacement, the history holds what was admitted, and the construction witness names the occurrences supplied. It reads no clock, performs no effect and allocates nothing not derived from the history.

Pydantic owns composition, constraints, aliases, unions, collections and validation. The declarations add only what Pydantic cannot state: the source of a record, its tie to an invocation, the closure of an evolving source, and which of two places a premise is obtained from.

## What is not here

A durable host. The history is in memory; nothing demonstrates recovery, worker ownership, stale commits, concurrency or early delivery against a store. Acceptance requirements N1 to N11 and N13 in [`docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../../../docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) need that host and remain unverified.

The binding constraints on this package are the reviewer's records under [`docs/decisions`](../../../docs/decisions/README.md), 0003 to 0007; [`0002`](../../../docs/decisions/0002-demand-admission-and-occurrence.md) and [`0001`](../../../docs/decisions/0001-durable-program-contract.md) are the experimental history they judge. What the evaluator does with a judgement it cannot read follows from the case analysis above, and its consequences are exercised: a condition written as a validator on a class whose premises two requests supply leaves both requests waiting and reports what was not established; the same condition written as a declared relation, or on a class whose premises one request supplies, progresses. A passive alternative beside an effect leaves the effect unchosen.

The package needs no runtime configuration.
