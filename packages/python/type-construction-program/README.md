# type-construction-program

Business programs as declared external relationships, the construction graph compiled from them, the recorded history of one invocation, and an experimental pure evaluation of the next justified work. This package is the executable form of the contract experiments under outcome 1 of [`TODO.md`](../../../TODO.md). It is experimental machinery for those experiments, not the replacement runtime, and the experiments do not resolve the design questions they exercise.

## What is here

- **Declarations** (`declaration`). `Source` names an external party. `Interaction` is one declared relationship with a source: what it says (`receives`), what this program sends to make it say so (`sends`), the receipt it may answer the send with first (`acknowledges`), how a delivery is tied to the invocation (`correlated`), where a premise of the request is obtained when its class alone does not say (`bound`), and the evidence after which it says nothing more (`closed_by`). `Binding` is that last declaration: a premise is obtained at a place in the accepted input, or a tuple follows a collection there. `Program` is a goal, the input it accepts, its interactions and its bindings; constructing one compiles the goal into a graph of construction relationships, `Node`, and refuses a program whose premise is obtained no way or more than one way, naming the places and the binding that would tell them apart, and one whose bindings contradict each other. `When[C, R]` is the one construction relationship a business type declares beyond its composition: its holder demands `R` only once `C` is constructed. It is a type and composes as types do: declared directly, under a business name that subclasses it, as a generic subclass, under `Annotated` metadata, or with premises of its own beside the two, it is read from the fields Pydantic resolves for the class and means the same; a conditional whose parameters are left open is refused, not read as a product. `C` and `R` are premises like any other, a class constructed through its own complete native boundary, an alternative or a collection. The condition is established in two operations the graph keeps apart: a candidate is obtained as any premise is, and the candidate is established through the conditional's complete declaration of its `condition` field, with every piece of metadata Pydantic resolved for it, a constraint, a validator, a normalization, a discriminator, under the conditional's configuration and the construction's context; so `When[Annotated[tuple[Line, ...], Field(min_length=2)], Shipped]` is established by two lines and never by one, and what a condition checks there is its own business. A conditional whose class validates its condition with a decorator is refused at declaration, since that check runs only when the class constructs, after the result; the check belongs in the condition's own type. A request occurrence is one send, the request an interaction sends constructed at one place; the records the source may answer it with are distinct propositions heard through that one send, not distinct sends, and the same operation at another collection's elements is another occurrence. Whether the condition can be established before what the conditional governs is reasoned over those occurrences, not operation names: a condition that every way it can be established passes through the very sends its result demands, and no other, is established only by the effects it governs, whichever way each is answered, and the program is refused naming the conditional, the operation and every place that demands an answer to it; a condition with another way, through one member of an alternative or the same operation at another occurrence, is a condition.
- **History and protocol** (`invocation`). `Invocation` is an append-only history: the accepted input, admitted `Evidence`, committed `Intended` effects and numbered `Attempted` outcomes. An occurrence is where a value comes from: a `Site`, a `Built` or `Elements`. `EffectIdentity` is the invocation, the request class and the occurrence of the request. `admissible` is the one judgement by which a claim becomes evidence, whether it came as a delivery (`admit`) or back inside an attempt (`perform`): selection by the request answered or the declared receive, validation, one claim per source and origin, every declared correlation holding together at one element, one answer per committed request, and closure, per element where the source is correlated into a collection. `acknowledge`, `failed` and `uncertain` record what else an attempt produced. The history owns its values: it copies the accepted input and each committed request, so the caller's objects remain the caller's and changing them afterwards changes no recorded premise, evaluation or effect identity; what it admits from a payload it constructs itself; what the evaluator derives, requests and the goal, are constructions of its own.
- **Evaluation** (`evaluation`). `evaluate(program, invocation)` returns `Completed`, `Frontier` or `Blocked`. A frontier holds the requests justified now, each with its `Derivation`, the receives outstanding, and the requests demanded but withheld: `Pending` while the condition of a conditional is open, or `Unchosen` because one live alternative needs it and another does not. Demand is propagated from the goal through the declared relationships, by three rules. A product is joint construction: every premise is demanded, the requests that supply them are dispatched together as soon as their own premises are there, and nothing orders one field before another, not a validator on the class or a field, not a constraint, not a source that may answer otherwise; a validator on a class that also holds an effect is a postcondition of that class, judged when it constructs, never prior authorization for the effect, and nesting, flattening or wrapping the same check changes nothing about what is demanded. A conditional is ordered demand: `When[C, R]` demands `C` first, through its own native construction, and `R` only once `C` is established: its candidate validated through the conditional's whole declaration of the condition, under the conditional's configuration and the refusal context, and that acceptance alone activates the result, with the candidate's occurrence as the witness; while `C` is open the operations under `R` are pending and none is constructed or proposed; if `C` is refused, by its own construction or by the condition's declaration, it is classified as the class owning the refusal classifies it, and nothing under `R` is ever demanded. The work that establishes `C` is justified by `C`'s own demand and needs its own premises like any other. An alternative demands what every live member needs, and withholds the rest as unchosen; a member the evidence refutes is no longer live. Each dispatched request's derivation names every path by which the goal demands it, the premise demanding the receive it answers and the conditions established on the way with the occurrence of each, and the revision of the history it was derived from; it is recorded where the request is proposed, not computed afterwards. A request is one construction whichever of the source's records a premise waits for, so two premises that need two different answers to one request demand one dispatch, with both paths, under one identity. A construction two places demand is constructed once and demanded twice, and its requests carry both paths, the conditional one only once its condition is established. Construction is native and complete: a class constructs through Pydantic from its constructed premises, by field name only, and nothing is judged before that, no constraint run on a field alone, no validator set aside, no fragment rebuilt from an annotation, so a padding before-validator and a revalidated element reading the context are accepted exactly as Pydantic accepts them; the one judgement made before a class constructs, a conditional's condition, is made the same way, the whole field declaration through Pydantic under the same context, and the class then constructs from the candidate, once, so a normalization is applied as the class applies it. A class with no premise, a constant or a relation over business constants, is constructed from nothing the same way, under the same context and with the same classification of its refusal, a root model's omitted root handed to Pydantic as its own omitted-root marker so the default applies natively. Construction is by node and scope, so two places that obtain a value the same way share one occurrence and two elements with equal content do not. It constructs from its own copy of each piece of evidence; a construction that alters its premise makes the evaluation `Blocked` as `Impure`. A validator that returns a replacement is native behaviour: the result holds the replacement, the history holds what was admitted, and the construction witness names the occurrences supplied. It reads no clock, performs no effect and allocates nothing not derived from the history.

Pydantic owns composition, constraints, aliases, unions, collections and validation. The declarations add only what Pydantic cannot state: the source of a record, its tie to an invocation, the closure of an evolving source, which of two places a premise is obtained from, and that one premise is demanded only once another is established.

## What is not here

A durable host. The history is in memory; nothing demonstrates recovery, worker ownership, stale commits, concurrency or early delivery against a store. Acceptance requirements N1 to N11 and N13 in [`docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../../../docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) need that host and remain unverified.

The binding constraints on this package are the reviewer's records under [`docs/decisions`](../../../docs/decisions/README.md), 0003 to 0007; [`0002`](../../../docs/decisions/0002-demand-admission-and-occurrence.md) and [`0001`](../../../docs/decisions/0001-durable-program-contract.md) are the experimental history they judge. The conditional construction `When` is the reviewer's amendment to the implicit-guard reading of those records, under which a sibling that might be refused ordered the effects beside it; here nothing is ordered that the declarations do not order, and the scenarios whose business requirement is conditional declare it. The consequences are exercised: the same condition as a validator, an annotated validator, a relation or a constraint inside a conditional withholds, refuses and releases alike; the same condition on a plain product orders nothing in any packaging; a passive alternative beside an effect leaves the effect unchosen. No program version is recorded yet; a derivation names the history's revision only.

The package needs no runtime configuration.
