# type-construction-program

Business programs as declared external relationships, the construction graph compiled from them, the recorded history of one invocation, and an experimental pure evaluation of the next justified work. This package is the executable form of the contract experiments under outcome 1 of [`TODO.md`](../../../TODO.md). It is experimental machinery for those experiments, not the replacement runtime, and the experiments do not resolve the design questions they exercise.

## What is here

- **Declarations** (`declaration`). `Source` names an external party. `Interaction` is one declared relationship with a source: what it says (`receives`), what this program sends to make it say so (`sends`), the receipt it may answer the send with first (`acknowledges`), how a delivery is tied to the invocation (`correlated`), where a premise of the request is obtained when its class alone does not say (`bound`), and the evidence after which it says nothing more (`closed_by`). `Binding` is that last declaration: a premise is obtained at a place in the accepted input, or a tuple follows a collection there. `Program` is a goal, the input it accepts, its interactions and its bindings; constructing one compiles the goal into a graph of construction relationships, `Node`, and refuses a program whose premise is obtained no way or more than one way, naming the places and the binding that would tell them apart, and one whose bindings contradict each other.
- **History and protocol** (`invocation`). `Invocation` is an append-only history: the accepted input, admitted `Evidence`, committed `Intended` effects and numbered `Attempted` outcomes. An occurrence is where a value comes from: a `Site`, a `Built` or `Elements`. `EffectIdentity` is the invocation, the request class and the occurrence of the request. `admissible` is the one judgement by which a claim becomes evidence, whether it came as a delivery (`admit`) or back inside an attempt (`perform`): selection by the request answered or the declared receive, validation, one claim per source and origin, every declared correlation holding together at one element, one answer per committed request, and closure, per element where the source is correlated into a collection. `acknowledge`, `failed` and `uncertain` record what else an attempt produced.
- **Evaluation** (`evaluation`). `evaluate(program, invocation)` returns `Completed`, `Frontier` or `Blocked`. A frontier holds the requests justified now, the receives outstanding, and the requests demanded but withheld: `Deferred` behind judgements still to be made on a premise beside it, or `Unchosen` because one live alternative needs it and another does not. Each open premise carries the judgements still to be made on it, at the places they are declared (`Judgement`): `Decided`, a source that may answer otherwise or a refutable condition; `Bounded`, a native constraint at a field; `Unread`, a validator, default or schema the evaluator locates but cannot read. A request whose result feeds no judgement decides nothing, so it waits for every judgement its conjunction has open. Requests feeding judgements the evaluator reads are dispatched together: each is the declared means of a decision the goal needs, and the declarations order none before another. An unread judgement grants nothing and settles nothing: what the effects beside it may do is derived by taking both of what it might be, a condition or an identity, and keeping only what holds either way. So a request feeding a readable decision is dispatched beside it; a request deciding nothing waits for it; a request feeding only the unread judgement waits for any readable decision beside it; and two unread judgements each resolved only by its own request leave both waiting, which, with nothing else to do, is reported as `Undetermined`, naming the judgements and the requests. `Deferred.behind` names what a request waits for and `Deferred.unread` whether that dependency is established or unresolved. A native constraint is judged before its class constructs only where that is Pydantic's own verdict (`early`): the field's metadata is native constraints alone and nothing before or around field validation can change it; otherwise it is judged by constructing, natively, and a judgement that can be made only by constructing the class that needs the withheld request is reported, not guessed. Construction is by node and scope, so two places that obtain a value the same way share one occurrence and two elements with equal content do not. It constructs pure values through Pydantic, by field name only, from its own copy of each piece of evidence; a construction that alters its premise makes the evaluation `Blocked` as `Impure`, and the history is never handed to a validator. A validator that returns a replacement is native behaviour: the result holds the replacement, the history holds what was admitted, and the construction witness names the occurrences supplied. It reads no clock, performs no effect and allocates nothing not derived from the history.

Pydantic owns composition, constraints, aliases, unions, collections and validation. The declarations add only what Pydantic cannot state: the source of a record, its tie to an invocation, the closure of an evolving source, and which of two places a premise is obtained from.

## What is not here

A durable host. The history is in memory; nothing demonstrates recovery, worker ownership, stale commits, concurrency or early delivery against a store. Acceptance requirements N1 to N11 and N13 in [`docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md`](../../../docs/REFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) need that host and remain unverified.

The binding constraints on this package are the reviewer's records under [`docs/decisions`](../../../docs/decisions/README.md), 0003 to 0007; [`0002`](../../../docs/decisions/0002-demand-admission-and-occurrence.md) and [`0001`](../../../docs/decisions/0001-durable-program-contract.md) are the experimental history they judge. Known limits of the current interpreter, stated here and not as decisions: a validator the evaluator cannot read is located, never interpreted, so a program whose every judgement is unread and whose judgements each need an effect is reported `Undetermined` rather than run; a native constraint at a field that also carries a callback, or in a class with a validator before field validation, is judged only by constructing, so where that class is the goal itself and needs the withheld effect the evaluator reports the judgement rather than make it; the sound fragment of early judgement is native constraint metadata alone, exercised against native construction in the tests and not proven in general; a passive alternative beside an effect leaves the effect unchosen.

The package needs no runtime configuration.
